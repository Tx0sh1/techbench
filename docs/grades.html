Grades & The Bench

Exactly how TechBench decides what's an A, B or C — and what happens to every device before you see it.

⚙️ Every unit runs the bench first

Before any device is listed, it goes through a full test pass on the workbench. The result is a bench certificate — a dated, unit-serialised record of every check that passed. You can open it for any unit on its product page.

The standard pass covers: screen & touch (dead pixels, lines), Face ID / Touch ID, cameras front and rear, speakers / mic / earpiece call test, charging port and wireless charging, WiFi / Bluetooth / cellular, buttons, haptics and sensors, liquid damage indicators, and a battery drain test. Battery health is read from the device itself — the real number, not an estimate.

✓ IMEI and iCloud status are checked before listing: no blacklisted units, no locked units, no iCloud-bound devices. Ever.

🏷️ The grades

A
Excellent — micro-scratches only You'll need to hunt for the flaws. Screen is clean, body is near-mint, everything works. This is the closest to new you'll get without paying new prices.
B
Honest signs of use Visible wear — light scuffs, a worn charging port surround, maybe a hairline mark on the frame. Nothing that affects function. The honest grade most resellers would quietly call "excellent".
C
Clear wear, fully working Real cosmetic wear: dents, scratches, scuffs — disclosed and photographed. Everything still passes the bench. Sold at a clear discount because of how it looks, not how it works.
The TechBench rule: we grade down, never up. If we're not sure whether it's an A or a B, it's a B. The certificate you see is the unit you get — same serial, same grade, same battery number.

📷 Pay after proof

We don't ask for a cent until you've seen what you're buying. The flow is always:

  • 1. You enquire on WhatsApp about a unit.
  • 2. We send real photos of that exact unit — not stock photos — plus its bench certificate.
  • 3. You confirm, we reserve the unit for you.
  • 4. You pay by EFT. We ship free, tracked, door to door.
  • 5. Your 30-day written warranty starts the day it lands.

Every sale is tied to a unit serial (TB-####). That serial never gets reused, and its certificate stays public even after the unit sells — so the history is always checkable.
