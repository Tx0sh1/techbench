Warranty & Returns

Version 1.0 · South Africa · Part of every sale

TechBench sells pre-owned/refurbished devices that are bench-tested before sale. This policy protects both the buyer and the seller.

Warranty length: 7 days from delivery — confirmed on your invoice before you pay. The cover rules below apply.

1. What every device comes with

  • A full bench test report: screen, touch, cameras, speakers, mic, charging, battery health %, WiFi/Bluetooth/cellular, Face ID/Touch ID, buttons, IMEI status.
  • A condition grade (A / B / C) with honest photos of the actual unit.
  • IMEI check confirming the device is not blacklisted and iCloud is removed.
  • A 7-day written warranty against functional defects from the delivery date — confirmed on your invoice.

2. What the warranty covers

  • Functional defects present at delivery that fail during the warranty period — e.g. screen failure, charging failure, camera failure, cellular/WiFi failure — provided the device has not been physically or liquid damaged.

3. What the warranty does NOT cover

  • Physical damage (drops, cracks, dents, bent frame)
  • Liquid damage (any moisture indicators tripped)
  • Battery degradation through normal use (battery health % is disclosed at sale)
  • Damage from unauthorized repair, tampering, or modified software
  • Loss, theft, or damage after delivery (buyer’s responsibility once received)
  • Cosmetic wear consistent with the disclosed grade

4. Warranty process

  1. Buyer contacts TechBench on WhatsApp within the warranty period with the invoice number and a description of the fault.
  2. TechBench may request a video/diagnostic to confirm the fault.
  3. If covered: repair or replacement is arranged. If repair/replacement is not possible, a refund of the purchase price (minus delivery) is issued.
  4. Buyer ships/returns the device at TechBench’s cost for covered faults. Buyer covers return shipping for non-covered faults.

5. Returns / cooling-off

  • Devices are sold as inspected — photos and condition report are part of the sale.
  • If the device does not match the condition report (grade, storage, IMEI, model), the buyer may return it within 7 days for a full refund — this is a misrepresentation return, and it is TechBench’s responsibility to make right.
  • Change-of-mind returns: accepted within 48 hours of delivery ONLY if the device is returned in the same condition, in original packaging, with all accessories — buyer covers return shipping and a 10% restocking fee applies.

6. Delivery

  • Delivery is free (Paxi / Courier Guy, door-to-door where available).
  • Risk passes to the buyer once the parcel is delivered (signed for).
  • Devices are packed securely with tracking; TechBench provides the tracking number.
  • Collection available in Mokopane — safe public meetup.

7. Consumer rights

  • Nothing in this policy limits the buyer’s rights under the South African Consumer Protection Act 68 of 2008. Where the CPA applies and provides a stronger remedy, the CPA prevails.
