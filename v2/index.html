Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the most common questions about buying used tech from TechBench SA.

Are any of your phones stolen or carrier-locked?

No. Every device sold by TechBench SA is checked using its IMEI and verified against stolen-device databases. We do not sell stolen, blacklisted, iCloud-locked, Google FRP-locked, or carrier-locked devices. Each phone is confirmed to be clean, unlocked, and ready to use with your own SIM before it is listed for sale.

Why are your used devices cheaper than buying new from iStore or other retailers?

TechBench SA focuses on high-quality used and professionally refurbished devices rather than brand-new stock. This means you pay a lower price because you are not paying for unopened packaging, retail overheads, or first-owner depreciation. We pass the savings on to you while still providing tested devices, clear grading, and a reliable warranty.

Do your used phones and devices come with a warranty?

Yes. Every device sold through TechBench SA includes a bench-tested warranty period covering hardware faults that were not disclosed at the time of sale. Cosmetic wear, battery condition, and grading notes are clearly explained before purchase. If a fault arises during the warranty period, we will repair, replace, or refund where applicable, in line with our terms and the Consumer Protection Act.

How does delivery work across South Africa?

We offer insured nationwide courier delivery for most devices. Orders are typically dispatched within 1–2 working days after proof of payment is received and the device is confirmed in stock. Delivery times vary by area, but most South African locations receive their order within 2–5 working days. Tracking is provided once your TechBench package is dispatched.

Can I pay by EFT, and how do I confirm payment?

Yes, EFT is accepted. Once your order is reserved, we will send the correct banking details, account reference, and order total. After making payment, send proof of payment to our WhatsApp or email address so we can confirm the order and arrange packing or dispatch. Please include your order number and full name with your payment reference.

Is collection available instead of delivery?

Yes, collection is available for approved orders. If collection is possible for your item, we will provide the pickup location, available times, and what you need to bring for verification. Collection is often the fastest option for urgent phones, laptops, tablets, and accessories, especially within major areas like Cape Town and Gauteng.
